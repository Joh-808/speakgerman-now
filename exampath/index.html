Your path in four phases

A steady eight weeks works for most people. Stretch or squeeze the middle phases to fit your exam date.

Week 1

Baseline

Sit one timed mock. Write down which of the four skills lost the most points.

Official Goethe mock exam (Modellsatz)
Weeks 2 to 4

Build

Work on vocabulary and grammar for the weakest skill first. Short daily sessions beat one long one.

Weeks 5 to 7

Drill

Go task type by task type below. Start with the parts worth the most points, and tick each one every time you practice it.

Week 8

Full mocks

Two complete timed exams. Review every wrong answer and rewrite one text per session.

Official Goethe practice exam (Übungssatz)

Task types by skill

Core marks the parts with the biggest share of a skill, or skills that carry over to other parts. Lower share marks the smallest part of a skill. Skills whose parts are all worth the same show no labels.

If you're scoring badly in a specific part, read its shortcut hints. They're what I've found to make the biggest difference in 2500h+ of teaching!
