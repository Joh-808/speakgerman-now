My first days in Germany · Part 1

Level A2 · 10 phrases · 6:52

Each phrase: German twice, then the English twice, then a soft gong, then German once for you to say.

Show all sentences
  1. 1Als ich in Deutschland ankam, hatte ich nur zwei Koffer.
    wört.: When I in Germany arrived, had I only two suitcases.
    engl.: When I arrived in Germany, I only had two suitcases.

    NOTE: als = one event or a period in the past · the verb goes to the end of the als-clause (ankam = ankommen).

  2. 2Es war sehr kalt, und es gab keinen Bus zum Hotel.
    wört.: It was very cold, and there was no bus to-the hotel.
    engl.: It was very cold, and there was no bus to the hotel.

    NOTE: es gab = there was / there were (one form for singular and plural).

  3. 3Der Taxifahrer hieß Ali und brachte mich zum Hotel.
    wört.: The taxi-driver was-called Ali and brought me to-the hotel.
    engl.: The taxi driver was called Ali and took me to the hotel.
  4. 4Die Nachbarn hießen Tom und Anna, und sie waren sehr nett.
    wört.: The neighbours were-called Tom and Anna, and they were very nice.
    engl.: The neighbours were called Tom and Anna, and they were very nice.
  5. 5Am nächsten Tag musste ich zum Amt gehen.
    wört.: On-the next day had-to I to-the office go.
    engl.: The next day I had to go to the government office.
  6. 6Die Frau am Schalter sagte, ich sollte eine Nummer ziehen.
    wört.: The woman at-the counter said, I should a number pull.
    engl.: The woman at the counter said I should take a number.
  7. 7Beim Warten dachte ich: „Das dauert ewig.“
    wört.: At-the waiting thought I: "That lasts forever."
    engl.: While waiting, I thought: "This is going to take forever."

    NOTE: beim Warten = bei + dem + warten · the verb becomes a noun (capital letter) = while waiting.

  8. 8Als der Mann mich etwas fragte, wusste ich nicht, was er wollte.
    wört.: When the man me something asked, knew I not, what he wanted.
    engl.: When the man asked me something, I didn't know what he wanted.
  9. 9Ich konnte kein Wort verstehen.
    wört.: I could no word understand.
    engl.: I couldn't understand a single word.
  10. 10Beim Ausfüllen fand ich das Formular schwer, und ich wurde nervös.
    wört.: At-the filling-out found I the form difficult, and I became nervous.
    engl.: While filling it in, I found the form difficult, and I got nervous.

My first days in Germany · Part 2

Level A2 · 10 phrases · 6:32

Each phrase: German twice, then the English twice, then a soft gong, then German once for you to say.

Show all sentences
  1. 11Zum Glück ging eine Frau mit mir zum Schalter.
    wört.: To-the luck went a woman with me to-the counter.
    engl.: Luckily, a woman went to the counter with me.
  2. 12Danach durfte ich das Formular zu Hause ausfüllen.
    wört.: Afterwards was-allowed I the form at home out-fill.
    engl.: After that, I was allowed to fill in the form at home.
  3. 13Wart ihr schon auf dem Amt?
    wört.: Were you(pl.) already at the office?
    engl.: Have you (all) been to the office yet?

    NOTE: ihr = you (plural, informal) · wart = past of sein for ihr.

  4. 14Ich habe dort fast nichts verstehen können.
    wört.: I have there almost nothing understand been-able.
    engl.: I was hardly able to understand anything there.

    NOTE: Perfekt with a modal verb: haben + two infinitives (verstehen können), not a participle (gekonnt).

  5. 15Ich habe dreimal fragen müssen.
    wört.: I have three-times ask had-to.
    engl.: I had to ask three times.
  6. 16Ich habe nicht so lange warten wollen.
    wört.: I have not so long wait wanted.
    engl.: I didn't want to wait that long.
  7. 17Als ich zur Arbeit gehen wollte, kam der Bus nicht.
    wört.: When I to-the work go wanted, came the bus not.
    engl.: When I wanted to go to work, the bus didn't come.
  8. 18Der Zug hatte zwanzig Minuten Verspätung, und mein Chef war nicht da.
    wört.: The train had twenty minutes delay, and my boss was not there.
    engl.: The train was twenty minutes late, and my boss wasn't there.
  9. 19Beim Einkaufen war der Supermarkt voll, und es gab keine Milch.
    wört.: At-the shopping was the supermarket full, and there was no milk.
    engl.: While I was shopping, the supermarket was packed, and there was no milk.
  10. 20Als ich endlich nach Hause kam, sagte ich: „Was für ein Tag!“
    wört.: When I finally to home came, said I: "What for a day!"
    engl.: When I finally got home, I said: "What a day!"
