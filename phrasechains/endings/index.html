Verb endings

Present tense phrase chains. Build each phrase from a clue, one step at a time: you hear it, you type it, you say it.

How it works

  1. 1Listen. You see and hear the start phrase, in English and in German.
  2. 2Build. You see and hear the next phrase in English, plus the words to use. Type it in German.
  3. 3Say it. Your German phrase joins the chain and is read out. Say it out loud, then the next clue appears.

Steps 2 and 3 repeat until the chain is finished. Tip: wear headphones and speak out loud.
